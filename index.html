FairN² Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

FairN² ("FairNSquare") is a judging app used by judges at HunterWiSE's student presentation night. It is developed by Fletcher Foggo as a University of Newcastle software engineering capstone project. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is handled.

Who can use the app

FairN² is only for judges invited by the event organisers. There is no public sign-up: organisers create each judge's account in a separate web-based admin panel and give the judge a username and 4-digit access code. The app is intended for adults (18+).

Information we collect

InformationWhy it's collected
Your nameEntered by the organiser so judges and results can be identified.
Username and access codeSet by the organiser so you can log in.
Scores and comments you enter for projectsThe core purpose of the app: calculating and reporting judging results.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

How information is used

  • To log you in and show the projects assigned to you.
  • To calculate results and give organisers a results export for the event.

Your information is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or marketing.

Sharing

Your name, scores and comments are visible to the event organisers (HunterWiSE) through the admin panel. Data is stored using third-party hosting providers (Render for the server and Neon for the database), located in Singapore, which process it only to run the service. It is not shared with anyone else.

Storage and security

  • All data is sent between the app and the server over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Access to judge data requires a valid login, and login attempts are rate-limited.
  • The app stores your login session on your device so you stay logged in; logging out removes it.

How long data is kept

Judging data is kept for as long as the organisers need it to finalise and record the event's results, and is then deleted. Test accounts used during app testing are deleted before the event.

Your choices and deleting your data

Because accounts are created and managed by the event organisers, you cannot delete your account from inside the app. To ask for your account, scores or comments to be deleted, or to ask what information is held about you, contact us at the address below and we will arrange it with the organisers.
